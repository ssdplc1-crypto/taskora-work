Privacy Policy

How TASKORA WORK handles information provided by users.

1. Information We Collect

TASKORA WORK may collect information required to create and operate your account, including your name, email address, phone number, account activity, task submissions, and bank account information provided for withdrawals.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage your account, process task submissions, calculate earnings, process withdrawals, verify accounts, provide support, and maintain platform security.

3. Task Proof

Screenshots and other proof submitted for tasks may be stored and reviewed by authorized TASKORA WORK administrators for the purpose of verifying completed work.

4. Payment Information

Payment and payout processing may involve third-party payment providers. TASKORA WORK uses information necessary to identify and process legitimate transactions.

5. Account Security

Users are responsible for protecting their passwords and account access. Do not share your password or login credentials with other people.

6. Information Sharing

TASKORA WORK does not use user information for purposes unrelated to operating, securing, supporting, or improving the platform, except where disclosure is required by law or necessary to provide a requested service.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information stored by the platform. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Services

TASKORA WORK may use third-party services for payment processing, hosting, infrastructure, or other operational requirements. Those services may process information according to their own policies.

9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updated versions will be made available through the TASKORA WORK platform.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact TASKORA WORK through the Support page.
