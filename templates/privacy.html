TASKORA WORK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

1. Information we collect

TASKORA WORK may collect information needed to create and operate an account, including your name, email address, phone number, account activity, task submissions, proof submitted for tasks, wallet records, bank details supplied for withdrawals, and payment-related transaction information.

2. How we use information

Information is used to provide the service, manage accounts, review task submissions, maintain wallet and transaction records, process withdrawals, prevent abuse, provide support, and maintain platform security.

3. Task proof

Images and other proof submitted with a task may be stored and made available to authorized administrators for the purpose of reviewing the submission.

4. Payments

Where a payment provider is used, relevant transaction information may be exchanged with that provider as necessary to verify payments or process supported transactions.

5. Security

TASKORA WORK takes reasonable measures to protect stored information. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so users should protect their passwords and account access.

6. Data sharing

Access to account and transaction information is limited to people and service providers who need it to operate, secure, support, or process the platform, subject to applicable requirements.

7. Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, transaction records, security, dispute handling, and other legitimate operational purposes.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated when the platform or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official TASKORA WORK support channel available through the platform.
