TASKORA WORK

Support

We are here to help with account, task, wallet and withdrawal issues.

Before contacting support

Please include the email or phone number connected to your TASKORA account and a clear description of the problem. Never send your password, Flutterwave secret key, OTP, PIN, or full card details.

Task issue

For a task problem, include the task title and explain what happened. If a submission was rejected, check the review note shown by the platform first.

Wallet or withdrawal issue

Include the withdrawal amount and the date of the request. Do not share sensitive banking credentials or authentication codes in a support message.

Payment issue

For activation/payment problems, provide the relevant transaction reference or non-sensitive payment information so the issue can be investigated.

Official support channel

Use the official TASKORA WORK support/contact channel provided by the platform. Do not trust unofficial accounts asking for your password, OTP, PIN, or secret keys.
